No sale available?

Modelled portfolio values assume you can sell at the chosen discount. A refused or delayed buy-back can leave you with no cash proceeds when needed. The price model cannot assign a defensible probability to that event.

“Zero log drift” is a modelling choice, not a flat-price or expected-zero-arithmetic-return guarantee. Same seed enables scenario comparisons.

04 / AUDIT TRAIL

Know what the numbers can tell you.

Data with its limitations attached

The interactive charts on SMI embed TRADIUM’s relative percentage series. We retrieved the MAX response for every listed metal and retain the original numeric JSON files and SHA-256 fingerprints. A 0% observation becomes an index of 100; it is not a zero metal price. The feed is rounded to whole percentage points.

All MAX series retrieved here stop on 22 April 2026. SMI’s November retail tables are separate snapshots. No invented prices or interpolated lines extend the history. Values at or below −100% cannot form a positive index; these provider observations are listed separately as unavailable and retained in raw files.

Monthly models use the last actual observation in each month and the longest consecutive common period across your chosen metals. In particular, the 2019 source gap is not treated as a single-month return. Earlier observations remain visible and downloadable; change the date window to study an earlier continuous period.

Transparent simulation assumptions

The Monte Carlo model draws circular blocks of consecutive monthly log returns. The same calendar block is drawn for every metal, preserving observed cross-metal dependence and some short-term clustering. At block boundaries it joins different historical regimes. This is a conditional scenario generator, not a calibrated prediction.

Monthly purchases follow your target weights; existing holdings are not rebalanced. Annual storage is deducted from NAV monthly. The sale discount applies once at liquidation. Optional tax is a simplified flat percentage of positive aggregate profit; it does not implement German tax rules or lot-by-lot holding exemptions.

Real profit compares final proceeds with each contribution compounded to the endpoint using your inflation assumption. The cash benchmark receives identical deposits and timing at the assumed cash rate. USD mode models dollar contributions and returns, rather than forecasting EUR/USD for a euro saver.

The interval around the simulated profit frequency is a 95% Wilson interval for Monte Carlo sampling error only. It does not capture parameter, valuation, liquidity or structural uncertainty. Even 100% winning sample paths cannot establish guaranteed future profit. Rolling windows overlap, and current basket selection uses hindsight.

Before placing an order

For this website, the published minimum is €10,000. At €150 a month, reaching that amount from zero takes 67 months before any interest. Fractional ownership does not establish that a €100–200 monthly plan is available. Confirm minimum top-ups and executable access directly.

SMI’s current FAQ describes doobloo AG, collective/fractional ownership and an insured Swiss bonded warehouse. Insurance against physical damage is different from price protection or a guaranteed buy-back. Its partner lists restrictions on gallium, germanium, dysprosium and terbium; indium prices are currently on request. Confirm current availability, ownership evidence, insolvency treatment, all costs and a same-day purchase/resale quote before investing.

doobloo publishes custody/insurance at 0.09% monthly of NAV (roughly 1.08% per year before compounding). This does not verify the contract you would receive through SMI. The app defaults to an illustrative 2% annual charge and 10% resale discount for sensitivity analysis.
