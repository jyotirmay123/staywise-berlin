## Reproducible arithmetic

The engine distinguishes income tax, solidarity and approximate church tax, and reports an income-tax marginal rate (finite smooth €100 increment for non-progression cases). The statutory full-euro floors apply to actual income-tax calculations. No claim that marginal rate equals the average tax paid.

Monthly loan payment = original principal × (nominal interest + initial repayment percentage) / 1200. Interest = opening balance × annual rate / 1200. The remaining payment repays principal. Extra repayment occurs in December after the regular payment and is capped at the remaining balance. Payments stop at payoff. Multiple loans are summed; interest and principal remain separate. Rate fixation expiry produces a warning, not an invented refinancing rate.

Rent grows on anniversaries of the configured rent-step interval. Parking and service receipts remain constant; expected vacancy scales all receipts. Monthly costs run inclusively from first to last month. Annual costs occur in the start month each year; one-off costs occur once. Expense inflation changes each anniversary. Inputs include expected legal/contractual increases but the app does not establish permission to raise rent.

Annual amounts are rounded to cents for property ledgers. Aggregate tax benefits are differences in modeled household liabilities; they are not flat marginal-rate multiplications. Each property is added in the selected attribution order and all favorable checks are recalculated. This ensures that the final total is independent of ordering, even when per-property benefits differ.

## What must be verified before a submission

Contracts and economic transfer date; ownership and usable rental share; deductible purpose of each loan; actual interest and rental receipts; paid expense classification and WEG year-end documents; reserve spending and capitalization; building/land value allocation and useful-life appraisal; actual insurance certificate amounts; household and pension eligibility. A starting example is not evidence. The app creates review worksheets and CSV attachments, not tax returns.

## Added payroll reconciliation

The app now has a limited regular-monthly-pay payroll branch separate from annual assessment. Primary specification: [BMF 2026 PAP](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/Steuern/Steuerarten/Lohnsteuer/Programmablaufplan/2025-11-12-PAP-2026.html), especially MPARA, MRE4JL, MZTABFB, UPEVP, MVSPKVPV, MVSPHB, UPLSTLZZ and UPANTEIL. [BMF XML source](https://bmf-steuerrechner.de/javax.faces.resource/daten/xmls/Lohnsteuer2026.xml.xhtml) was read during implementation. The employee basic-health payroll rate is7% plus half insurer Zusatzbeitrag; the payroll special-expense lump sum is€36 even for classIII. These payroll parameters must not be substituted for final joint-assessment deductions.

[2026 LStH, §39a, R39a.1(7)](https://gewsth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/VI-Steuererhebung-36-47/2-Steuerabzug-vom-Arbeitslohn-Lohnsteuer-38-42g/Paragraf-39a/inhalt.html) requires rounding monthly Freibeträge upward to full euros. The actual observed monthly amount takes precedence in the reference month. An additional-property preview adjusts that applied baseline and is explicitly unapproved. Annual final tax is never reduced by both the Freibetrag and the modeled rental loss.

Supported payroll scope: 2026 regular monthly salary, statutory RV/AV/GKV/PV, classesI/III/IV without factor, under64, no pensioner exemptions, bonus calculation or child-allowance adjustment for surcharges. Unsupported classes are rejected. This reproduces the documented reference month; it does not certify all payroll cases or future years.

## Risk framework and active-only selection

The risk score is our disclosed planning heuristic, not a scientific/default-probability model. Broad supporting consumer guidance: [Verbraucherzentrale, inability to meet property instalments](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/geld-versicherungen/bau-und-immobilienfinanzierung/immobilienfinanzierung-was-tun-wenn-sie-raten-nicht-mehr-zahlen-koennen-46653). That source supports budgeting, reserves and rate/payment sensitivity; it does NOT validate our score weights or any purchase recommendation.

Weights: reserve months25, free-cash margin25, rental debt coverage15, debt/purchase-cost proxy15, combined downside headroom20. Threshold choices are shown in the UI. A negative base year, an annual combined funding deficit above available cash, or an immediate repair hit above that cash caps the score at39. Less than three reserve months or a fundable negative combined year caps it at59. The repair hit is counted once in annual combined deficits; it is not subtracted from cash a second time for that comparison. Overall score is the minimum of ten yearly scores.

Unknown personal inputs and active-property confirmations block a score; partially known exposures can still be inspected. User-choice stress parameters are not forecasts. Nonzero pay shocks replace manual insurance deductions with statutory estimates; zero shocks preserve the entered baseline. Extra-rate sensitivity begins after exact fixation-end month when provided, otherwise after the entered fixation years; first affected years are prorated. Principal repayment pace is held constant, so this is not an actual refinancing quote.

Only enabled properties and strategies enter analysis. Confirmed guarantee premiums are inserted exactly once; unknown terms provide no risk credit. The ChatGPT report uses an explicit whitelist of effective state, and disabled names, contributions and historical property allowances are excluded.
